# PRIVACY POLICY

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information this website and its features (the “Site”) collect and how it is used. It should be read together with the Site’s Terms of Service (the “Terms”). Terms defined in the Terms, including “Site,” “User Content,” “Site operator,” and “developers,” have the same meaning here.

## 2. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you register or sign in, the Site collects your email address and a password. Passwords are stored as hashes, not in readable form.

**Verification information.** To confirm that a user belongs to the school community, the Site uses join codes, onboarding codes, QR codes, and signed tokens. The Site stores the information needed to operate this system, including the codes and tokens and their link to a user account.

**Role information.** The Site assigns roles to accounts and records role approvals.

**User Content.** The Site stores the User Content you submit, including edit requests, and records connected to who submitted it.

**Technical information.** As with any website, your IP address and basic request details are received by the servers and network services that deliver the Site, including Cloudflare, which routes traffic to the Site.

## 3. CAPTCHA (Cap)

The Site uses a CAPTCHA service called Cap to help block automated abuse. Cap is run by the Site operator as its own service, with its own separate temporary data store. When the Cap widget is shown, which depends on the Site’s settings, your browser contacts the Cap service and sends the technical information described in section 2 along with the data needed to complete the challenge.

## 4. How we use information

Personal information is used to create and manage accounts, verify that users belong to the school community, assign and enforce roles, display and review User Content, and protect the Site against abuse.

## 5. Who can see information

Content you submit through a feature that publishes content may be visible to other users or to visitors of the Site. As described in the Terms, the Site operator may refer concerns to school officials or appropriate authorities. Cloudflare handles Site traffic as described in section 2.

## 6. Changes and contact

The Site operator may update this policy by publishing a revised version on the Site. Questions, complaints, or requests about this policy or your information should be directed to the Site operator or the school through the contact information published on the Site.
